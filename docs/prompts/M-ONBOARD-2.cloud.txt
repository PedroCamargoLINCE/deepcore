THIS IS A WRITING BRIEF WITH A MACHINE ATTACHED, NOT THE REVERSE. Roughly half
your effort belongs in the authored lines. The tutorial is most players' first
impression of the game and the director wants it to be the reason they tell
someone about it. A correct tutorial that is not funny has failed this brief.

THE BIT. GRIT teaches, loudly, and Law 3 (GRIT never explains) is suspended
here BY the fiction rather than in spite of it: he has been handed the Company
induction script and is contractually obliged to read it out, and he cannot get
through a line without riffing on it. He hates this. That tension is the whole
joke engine — the flat corporate line, then GRIT on the flat corporate line.

  "Step one. 'Place the extractor upon the ore.' Upon it. Not near it. They put
   that in writing because someone tried near."

REGISTER — the whole range, deliberately mixed, per the director verbatim:
"I WANT MY MAN FOREMAN TEACHING AND MAKING JOKES! ... AND CHILD JOKES! AND DAD
JOKES! I DONT GIVE A FUCK IF PLAYERS NEED TO PARSE A JOKE!"

  - dad jokes and groaners, delivered with total confidence
  - the joke a nine-year-old makes about a drill going into a hole
  - filthy adult ones
  - puns he is visibly proud of and will not apologise for
  He should be funny AND a bit embarrassing — a man twenty years down a hole
  with nobody to talk to. Aim for ~60 AUTHORED LINES so a second playthrough is
  not the same script.

Guardrails are unchanged and are where the humour gets better, not weaker:
profanity, innuendo, gallows humour and Company-hating are the point; slurs,
hate, explicit anatomy and non-consent framing are out. test/barks_test.js
enforces the voice lint and the line-overlap ceiling — every new line passes it.

STRUCTURE
- Trigger on a profile with zero completed runs. Skippable from the first
  keypress, and never shown again once skipped or finished (persist in meta,
  with a default that keeps older saves loading — add a test for that).
- Teach by DOING, on the real generated map, not a scripted sandbox. Four
  beats, each gated on the player actually performing it: place an extractor on
  a vein · rotate its output · lay a belt to the rig · watch the first ore
  arrive. Stratum 1 already guarantees an iron vein near the rig.
- Instability does not climb until the first ore reaches the rig. Nobody loses
  their first run to a quake while reading — least of all to a punchline.

PRESENTATION — centre stage, not the foreman bar. The two-row bar at the bottom
is for ambient barks; a tutorial delivered there is missable and cannot feel
dynamic. Build a centred panel or a popup that appears NEAR what he is talking
about, points at it, lands, and gets out of the way. M-HIRES's sub-cell block
rasterizer (DC.Art.raster) can give the box a real tail/pointer without leaving
the character grid. Honor §0 law 6: the box must never cover the thing it is
pointing at, and must stay legible at every viewport scale M-HIRES supports.

THE ONE THING THAT MUST SURVIVE THE COMEDY. However dumb the joke, the ACTION
is unmissable on screen at the same time: the key drawn as an inverse keycap,
and the target tile highlighted. This is not a limit on the humour — it is what
LETS the humour be as stupid as it likes, because the player never has to parse
a gag to find out what to press. A beat where the joke is the only instruction
gets rewritten.

DETERMINISM (§0 law 7). Tutorial line choice must not spend from run.rng —
pick by seed hash or roll once in the game loop and store it on the run. The
tutorial runs on a live run whose map and schematics depend on that stream.

- A save written before this brief still loads, and a profile that has finished
  or skipped the tutorial never sees it again.
- barks_test extended over the new pool: voice lint, overlap ceiling, and the
