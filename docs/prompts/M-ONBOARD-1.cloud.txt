WHY THIS BRIEF EXISTS. The director could not play his own game while every
test suite was green. Reproduce it before you fix it, because the reproduction
is the acceptance test:

  place an extractor on a revealed ore vein, with NO belt on its output side,
  and tick the sim 600 times.

It mines an ore on the first cycle, puts it in machine.out, finds nowhere to
put it, sets status 'blocked', and sits there forever. It emits ZERO log
messages. render.js draws a red '!' over it (see the `bad` branch around
js/render.js:638) and nothing anywhere says what that '!' means. A player sees
a machine do nothing plus one unexplained mark and concludes the game is
broken. The mechanics are sound; the teaching is absent.

WHAT TO BUILD

1. Every failure state names its own cause, ONCE, in the LOG:
   - blocked output (the case above) — say what is wrong AND the fix, e.g.
     "The extractor is full. Nothing is carrying it away — lay a belt on its
     output side (R rotates before you place)."
   - out of fuel, depleted vein, jammed belt, an assembler with no recipe or
     no inputs, and the rig when a breach stalls for want of a specific item.
   - Once per run per CAUSE, not per machine and not per tick. A message that
     repeats is a message players learn to ignore.

2. Disambiguate '!'. It currently means blocked OR nofuel OR depleted with no
   way to tell which. Either differentiate the glyph or — better — make the
   SCAN panel state the reason when the cursor or pointer is on the machine.
   That panel already exists and already has room. Whatever you choose must
   pass the contrast and width rules the existing panels are held to.

VOICE. This is the SYSTEM's voice, not GRIT's. Law 3 says GRIT never explains
mechanics, and law 6 says legibility beats everything — so the log line must be
plainly, boringly clear FIRST. GRIT may editorialize on top afterwards (a
separate bark, and only for the first occurrence in a run) but he must never be
the one carrying the information. If a player has to parse a joke to learn why
their base is stopped, the brief has failed.

DETERMINISM. These messages originate in the sim, so they are sim events: no
Date.now, no Math.random, and no draws from run.rng (law 7 — derive a stream
from the seed if you need one at all, though this brief probably needs none).
Two runs with the same seed and the same inputs must produce the same messages
in the same order.
